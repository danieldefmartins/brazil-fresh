02 The brand

Fresh earth, carried to the world

Terra Fresca Trading is a produce trading house. We buy fruit and vegetables directly from certified Brazilian growers, take ownership of every container, run the cold chain and the paperwork ourselves, and sell wholesale to importers, distributors and retail chains worldwide.

We are not a carrier and not a commission broker. One seller, one invoice, one accountable team.

What the name means

  • Terra — the earth, the farm, the origin.
  • Fresca — fresh, alive, arriving in condition.
  • Reads correctly in Portuguese, English, Spanish and Italian.
  • Legal name of the trading arm: Terra Fresca Trading.
  • Future retail arm: Terra Fresca Market — same mark, terracotta accent.

03 The mark

One monogram, three lockups

01 · Horizontal — primary
02 · Stacked — covers & print
03 · Monogram — icon, app, stamp

The horizontal lockup is the default — use it wherever width allows: letterheads, invoices, footers, trade-show banners. The stacked lockup is for square and portrait spaces. The monogram stands alone only where the name already appears nearby, or at small sizes: favicon, social avatar, container door, crate stamp.

The T carries the F; the gold leaf is the produce between them03
04 Backgrounds

Full colour on light. Reversed on dark.

Cream / white — full colour
Navy — reversed
Forest — reversed
Monogram reversed

Clear space

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the T bar on all sides. Nothing — type, photo edge, page trim — enters that space.

Minimum size

Horizontal lockup: 34 mm / 130 px wide. Monogram: 10 mm / 28 px wide. Below that, the leaf closes up — use the monogram, never the shrunk lockup.

05 Protecting the mark

Never do this

Don't recolourOnly navy/forest/gold in full colour, or the reversed cream version. No gradients, no single-colour green mark, no brand-colour swaps.
Don't distortNever stretch, squash, rotate, skew, or add outline, shadow, bevel or glow.
Don't rebuildNever re-typeset the wordmark in another font or re-space the letters. Always use the supplied files.
Don't crowdNo text, icon or image inside the clear space; never lock the mark to another logo without a divider rule.
Don't place on noiseOn photography, use the reversed mark over a solid navy panel or a darkened area — never over busy fruit texture.
Don't shrink the lockupBelow minimum size switch to the monogram. A lockup with an unreadable tagline is worse than no lockup.

08 Voice

How we sound

We are

  • Specific. "Santos to Rotterdam in 11 days" — not "fast global logistics".
  • Accountable. We own the container, so we speak in commitments.
  • Plain. A buyer in Rotterdam reads English as a second language. Short sentences win.
  • Proud of origin. Brazil is the product, never an apology.

We are not

  • Not a carrier, freight forwarder or broker — never imply we move other people's cargo.
  • No hype adjectives: "world-class", "cutting-edge", "synergy".
  • No promises we can't log: never claim a temperature or a date we don't control.

Approved lines

From Brazilian soil. To tables worldwide.
